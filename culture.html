1. APSARA DANCE

Traditional Khmer Dance

Apsara Dance is an important part of Cambodia culture. It represents the beauty of Khmer classical art. The Dancers wear beautiful traditional customes and perform graceful movements.

2. KHMER NEW YEAR

Khmer New Year is one of the most important celebrations in Cambodia

People celebrate by visiting temples, playing traditional games, and spending time with their families.

3. TRADITONAL KHMER FOOD

Fish Amok

Fish Amok is a famous Cambodian traditional dish. It is prepared with fish, coconut milk, and Khmer spices.

Many Cambodians consider it a special traditional food.
